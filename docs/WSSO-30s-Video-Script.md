# WSSO 30-Second Sales Video Script  
## Total Vision Management System (AI-generated video)

**Length:** 30 seconds max  
**Format:** Voiceover + on-screen text + real WSSO dashboard screenshots  
**Audience:** US business owners / operators (prospect clients)  
**Tone:** Fast, confident, money-first — then show the system  

**Prepared for:** Terence L. Bolden / WSSO go-to-market  
**Date:** August 2026  

---

## 1. Quick glossary (say this once in the video — short)

| Term | What it means (client-friendly) | What to show on screen |
|---|---|---|
| **Total Vision Management System** | One AI-enabled command center so leaders see what is happening **inside their organization** (and market/competitive signals) — work, people, pipeline — customized to daily workflow | Role dashboards (Admin / Manager / Employee) |
| **Role dashboards** | **Admin** = whole-organization control · **Manager** = team command center · **Employee** = personal daily work view | Triple-cut of the 3 dashboards |
| **TACTICs** | Structured meeting → action docs: decisions, owners, tasks, next steps — so meetings turn into revenue work, not notes that die | **TACTICs** list / one TACTIC detail |
| **SMART Goals** | Goals that are **Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound** — tied to live work orders & deadlines on the board | **Work Orders** or **Kanban** with due dates |
| **SPEEDY** | The execution rhythm: move from goal → tactic → done **fast** — less stalling, less overhead, more closes in less time *(confirm official letter-meanings with Terence if he has a fixed acronym)* | Kanban moving a card / CRM leads |

> **Note:** In the product today, **role-based dashboards**, **TACTICs**, and work orders are live in WSSO. **Total Vision / SMART / SPEEDY** are the sales story that wraps those screens. If Terence has official SPEEDY letter definitions, swap the SPEEDY line in Section 3.

## 2. Value proposition (must hit in first 3 seconds)

**Hook promise:**  
Save **money**, **time**, and **staffing cost** — while **generating more revenue** with less daily overhead.

**One-line VP:**  
WSSO’s Total Vision Management System gives every role its own dashboard (Admin / Manager / Employee), runs work through TACTICs + SMART goals at SPEEDY pace, and drives acquisition/sales with AI — **more revenue, less waste**.

## 10. What NOT to do in 30 seconds

- Don’t narrate a full tour of every Admin menu — **one Admin SS + label is enough**  
- Don’t define every SMART letter out loud — show them on screen  
- Don’t soft-open (“Hi, today we’d like to introduce…”) — **money hook first**  
- Don’t skip role dashboards — Terence wants Admin / Manager / Employee visible
